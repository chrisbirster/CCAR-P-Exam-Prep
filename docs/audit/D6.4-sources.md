## 4. Direct evidence vs. interpretation

**Directly supported by exam scope:**
- document architecture;
- provide implementation guidance.

**Architecture interpretation:**
- our ADR field list;
- context/data-flow/trust-boundary diagrams;
- runbooks, eval definitions, risk-control matrices, dashboards, rollback plans;
- document decisions that are expensive/risky/hard to reverse;
- keep docs close to the version they describe.

## 5. Current note under review

Our note teaches documentation that preserves rationale, assumptions, alternatives, ownership, risks, and operational guidance—not just diagrams.
