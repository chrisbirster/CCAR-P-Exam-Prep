## 2. Primary source — Multi-Agent Research System

[Multi-Agent Research System](../references/multi-agent-research-system.md)

Anthropic describes a lead/orchestrator agent delegating independent research tasks to subagents, with parallelism, specialization, separate trajectories, and coordination challenges.

## 3. Primary source — Effective Context Engineering

[Effective Context Engineering](../references/effective-context-engineering.md)

Anthropic discusses context isolation as a reason to separate work into subagents.

## 4. Direct evidence vs. interpretation

**Directly supported:**
- orchestrator-worker pattern;
- parallel subagents;
- context isolation;
- coordination cost/failure modes.

**Architecture interpretation:**
- our required worker contract fields such as timeout/retry/disagreement policy;
- "three strong reasons" taxonomy: isolation, parallelism, specialization;
- state-ownership recommendations.

## 5. Current note under review

Our note says multi-agent systems are justified by meaningful isolation, parallelism, or specialization—not by novelty—and require explicit contracts, traceability, and synthesis checks.
