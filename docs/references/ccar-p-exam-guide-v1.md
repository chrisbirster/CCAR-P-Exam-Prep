# CCAR-P Exam Guide v1.0

**Source type:** Official exam blueprint / certification scope  
**Effective:** July 2026  
**Verified:** September 26, 2026

## Why this source matters

This is the highest-priority source for deciding **what is in scope for the exam**.

The study repository uses the published CCAR-P v1.0 structure:

- D1 Solution Design & Architecture — 17%
- D2 Claude Models, Prompting & Context Engineering — 13%
- D3 Integration — 19%
- D4 Evaluation, Testing & Optimization — 16%
- D5 Governance, Safety & Risk Management — 14%
- D6 Stakeholder Communication & Lifecycle Management — 14%
- D7 Developer Productivity & Operational Enablement — 7%

Together these domains contain 38 task statements.

## What it supports

This reference supports the **existence and scope** of every D1.1–D7.3 study concept.

It does **not** mean every explanatory sentence in the study notes is verbatim Anthropic exam guidance. Technical explanations are separately grounded in the first-party references in this folder.
