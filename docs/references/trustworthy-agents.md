# Safe and Trustworthy Agents

**Publisher:** Anthropic

## Humans remain in control

Anthropic's trustworthy-agent framework emphasizes maintaining meaningful human control as agents become more capable and consequential.

**Supports:** D5.3.

## Secure interactions

Agents can be attacked through prompt injection or vulnerabilities in tools/subagents, motivating layered security.

**Supports:** D5.1, D5.2.

## Transparency

Anthropic includes transparency as a core trustworthy-agent principle.

**Supports:** D5.5.

## Privacy

Protecting privacy is part of the stated framework.

**Supports:** D5.4, D5.5.

## Risk grows with consequential action

As agents receive access to more valuable data and actions, security and oversight requirements increase.

**Supports:** D3.1, D5.2, D5.3.

## Architecture interpretation

The exact form of HITL—pre-approval, post-review, or sampling—depends on consequence, reversibility, regulation, and product requirements. That decision framework is our architecture interpretation built on the broader human-control principle.
