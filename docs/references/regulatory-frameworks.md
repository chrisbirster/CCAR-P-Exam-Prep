# Regulatory Framework References

**Verified:** September 26, 2026

This page exists because D5.4 explicitly names GDPR, HIPAA, and FedRAMP. It is a **navigation and architecture reference**, not legal advice and not a substitute for counsel or a compliance assessment.

## GDPR

**Primary legal text:** Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR)  
https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj

Architecture topics that may be affected include personal-data processing, minimization, retention, access, security, data-subject workflows, and international transfer requirements.

Do not turn a simplified study note into a claimed legal rule without checking the regulation and applicable guidance.

## HIPAA

**Primary U.S. HHS portal:**  
https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/index.html

Architecture topics can include protected health information, access controls, safeguards, auditability, minimum-necessary handling, and relationships with service providers.

HIPAA applicability is fact-specific; exam study should focus on the blueprint's architecture implications rather than pretending a short flashcard is compliance advice.

## FedRAMP

**Official program:**  
https://www.fedramp.gov/

Architecture topics can include authorization boundaries, control implementation/inheritance, evidence, continuous monitoring, change management, and use of authorized cloud services.

## Study rule

For CCAR-P:

```text
regulatory requirement
→ architecture/control
→ owner
→ evidence
→ review/monitoring
```

Use the exam guide to determine exam scope and the actual primary regulatory/program source when exact legal or control requirements matter.

**Supports:** D5.4.
