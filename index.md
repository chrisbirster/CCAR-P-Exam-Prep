## The Architecture Mindset

When you see a scenario, work through it in this order:

```text
Business outcome
      ↓
Constraints
      ↓
Responsibility split
      ↓
Architecture pattern
      ↓
Model + context
      ↓
Integrations + permissions
      ↓
Safety + governance
      ↓
Evaluation
      ↓
Operations + observability
      ↓
Ownership + business value
```

A useful default question is:

> **What is the simplest architecture that satisfies the stated quality, security, safety, compliance, latency, reliability, and cost constraints?**

## Recommended Study Order

### 1. Solution Design & Architecture
Build the decision framework first.

### 2. Integration
Learn how Claude connects to enterprise data, tools, and systems safely.

### 3. Evaluation, Testing & Optimization
Learn how to prove an architecture works rather than assuming it does.

### 4. Models, Prompting & Context Engineering
Optimize the Claude-specific parts of the architecture.

### 5. Governance, Safety & Risk
Add enforceable controls and explicit risk ownership.

### 6. Stakeholder Communication & Lifecycle
Learn how designs survive handoff, production, and organizational change.

### 7. Developer Productivity & Operational Enablement
Help engineering teams use and operate the system without depending on the original architect.

## How to Study

### Explain
Can you describe the concept without notes?

### Compare
Can you distinguish it from plausible alternatives?

### Decide
Can you select an architecture under constraints and defend the trade-off?

For CCAR-P, **Decide** is the level that matters most.
